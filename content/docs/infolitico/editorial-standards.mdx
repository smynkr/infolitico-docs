Infolitico's editorial standards are not style guidelines — they are enforced
rules. The enforcement lives in the pipeline's code and database policies, not
in model prompts, so a careless or hostile model cannot talk its way past a
safety rule.

## The hard rules

### 1. Never fabricate or modify Bible verse text

Every verse is reproduced **exactly** from the curated `bible_verses`
database. The pipeline never writes a verse from memory, never paraphrases a
quoted verse, and never edits the wording of a curated verse. The verified
NLT block is the only full-verse quotation in an article.

### 2. No fabricated quotes attributed as real

Commentary quotes are fetched from the `commentary_quotes` table and attached
to the article **in code** between pipeline stages — the outline and draft
models receive them as verified input and cannot emit their own quote text.
At most one quote may appear in an article, verbatim and attributed.

### 3. Protected people and minors are database-owned policy

Policy about protected people and minors is enforced by the database and the
pipeline's code, not by asking a model to "remember the rules." A
database-owned ruleset checks every story against minors-centered vocabulary
and age expressions — before any LLM work begins and again after the article
is drafted, immediately before publish. An evaluation error or an empty
ruleset fails the operation rather than being treated as a clear result. A
pre-publish person check also runs before any story can publish.

The rules cover unambiguous minors vocabulary (child, children, teen,
schoolchild, infant, toddler, and similar terms), bounded youth-program
language, and explicit under-18 and age-expression forms. They deliberately
omit broad terms like bare "minor," "youth," "school," or "student" so
ordinary adult reporting is not rejected. Rules live in the database so
policy maintenance does not require weakening a code guard.

### 4. The last-line content gate

`polish-and-guard` is the final content gate before publication. Combined
with editorial review, headline-novelty checks, the protected-content
evaluation, and the pre-publish person check, a story must clear every gate
or it is rejected — never patched by prompt.

### 5. Editorial accountability: retractions and corrections

When a published story is later found to violate a standard or contain an
error, Infolitico uses an append-only editorial-action ledger. Every
retraction and correction is version-checked, idempotent, and recorded with
a public notice.

- **Retracted stories** show a "This story is no longer available" notice at
  their original URL instead of disappearing silently.
- **Corrected stories** display a correction notice above the article.
- Internal details (operator references, request hashes, reason codes) are
  never exposed publicly; only the action kind, public notice, and timestamp
  are shown to readers.

## News first, faith second

The article leads with accurate, concise reporting; the spiritual layer rides
on top and never replaces it. The pipeline's source-evidence preflight rejects
events that cannot be factually summarized, and the faith-angle stage rejects
events with no genuine faith connection. A story is never forced into a
devotional frame.

## Voice, bounded

- **Warm and conversational** — a thoughtful friend sharing a morning
  devotional, not a pastor at a pulpit.
- **Values, not politics** — family, personal responsibility, stewardship,
  gratitude, character, community, and faith in God's plan. Never party
  names, candidate endorsements, or left/right framing.
- **Earnest and sincere** — no irony, no hedging, no academic distance.
- **Short and accessible** — 300–500 words, a two-minute read.

<Callout type="warn">
  Rejection is the pipeline's primary safety mechanism. If a story cannot be
  reported accurately, connected genuinely to faith, and cleared by every
  gate, it is not published.
</Callout>
