[The Feed](https://infolitico.com/feed) is a calm, bounded window into public
conversations about faith, culture, news, and civic life. Infolitico selects a
fixed allowlist of Reddit communities, verifies the posts in a worker, and
serves a cached snapshot. It does not turn the Feed into a second newsroom or
endorse the claims in a post.

## Curated views

The Feed has five named views. Every view uses the same bounded, verified public
snapshot; changing views changes the selected allowlist, not the underlying
verification or disclosure rules.

| View                        | Communities included                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **The Daily Mix**           | `r/Christianity`, `r/Catholicism`, `r/religion`, `r/news`, and `r/NeutralPolitics`                                                                                                         |
| **Faith communities**       | The eight faith communities in the allowlist: `r/Christianity`, `r/Catholicism`, `r/Reformed`, `r/TrueChristian`, `r/OrthodoxChristianity`, `r/Bible`, `r/AskAChristian`, and `r/religion` |
| **News communities**        | `r/news`, `r/worldnews`, `r/LocalNews`, and `r/UpliftingNews`                                                                                                                              |
| **Civic communities**       | `r/NeutralPolitics`, `r/moderatepolitics`, `r/PoliticalDiscussion`, `r/PoliticalScience`, `r/Economics`, and `r/AskHistorians`                                                             |
| **All curated communities** | All 18 communities in the faith, news, and civics allowlist                                                                                                                                |

The worker requests each selected community's top posts for the day, verifies
candidate posts against Reddit's embed, and keeps at most 24 verified posts in a
snapshot. A refresh can be delayed or unavailable when a community fails or a
candidate does not verify; an individual post page is unavailable after its
stored expiry time.

## Freshness and empty states

The Feed labels a snapshot as **Verified public snapshot**, **Some listings
delayed**, or **Listings temporarily unavailable**. A delayed label means the
last verified snapshot is being served while the next refresh is late. When no
usable verified posts are available, the page says so and shows no sample or
invented posts. That fail-closed behavior is intentional.

## External content disclosure

Posts and excerpts come from public Reddit feeds and remain the responsibility
of their authors. Infolitico curates the community allowlist, not individual
posts, and does not endorse their claims or viewpoints.

Opening a Feed item first takes you to an Infolitico detail and discussion page.
That page repeats the disclosure, shows when the post was last verified, and
links to the linked source when present and to the original Reddit post. The
local conversation is hosted by Infolitico and is separate from Reddit; comments
never write back to Reddit.

## Reader discussion and sign-in

You can browse the public snapshot without an account. Reading or joining the
Infolitico-hosted conversation requires reader sign-in. Choose **Sign in** on a
post's discussion panel, or open `/login`, enter an email address, and request a
sign-in link. The sign-in page says that the email address is never shown to
other readers.

Signed-in readers can read the discussion and use the available comment
controls. Public comments use a database-derived pseudonym rather than the
reader's email address. Read the [Community Standards](/infolitico/policies#community-standards)
for participation rules, reporting, moderation, and appeals.

## One shared edition

The Feed is separate from the shared Infolitico edition. Infolitico does not
change story selection or ordering based on where you arrived from, a political
label, or a personal profile. The same published story pool is available to
every reader, and the system uses source provenance, themes, sections, and
freshness to improve coverage without turning a visit into a political signal.

Read [How the Edition Works](/infolitico/policies#shared-edition) for the full
personalization statement, or return to the [Front Page](https://infolitico.com/)
for the current edition.
