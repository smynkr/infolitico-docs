Infolitico's primary navigation is a set of distinct reading desks. The site is
free to browse: open a section, choose a story, or move through the archive
without creating an account. The separate [Feed discussion](/infolitico/the-feed)
is the exception; reading or joining a Feed conversation requires reader
sign-in.

## The primary navigation

| Destination                          | What you will find                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Front Page** (`/`)                 | The current shared Infolitico edition. It is the homepage's canonical edition, with the latest story sections and the newsletter signup.                                     |
| **Faith & Church** (`/faith-church`) | Worship, ministry, and communities of faith shaping public life. Its topics include ministry, worship and practice, faith and public life, formation, and the global church. |
| **Verse Index** (`/verse-index`)     | The curated Bible verse library, organized by theme. A verse that appears in a published reflection links to its latest related story.                                       |
| **Thoughts** (`/thoughts`)           | Longer, source-grounded reflections on the events of the day. Scripture appears when a natural connection is present, and the original source remains part of the reading.   |
| **Nation** (`/nation`)               | Stories of communities, volunteers, and neighbors living out generosity across the country.                                                                                  |
| **Business** (`/business`)           | Stories of enterprise, innovation, and people stewarding resources while building for the future.                                                                            |
| **Culture** (`/culture`)             | Books, films, music, technology, and the ideas that shape how believers see the world.                                                                                       |
| **World** (`/world`)                 | International developments and stories that connect readers to communities and peoples around the world.                                                                     |
| **Politics** (`/politics`)           | Government, public service, and the leaders shaping communities and the wider world.                                                                                         |
| **The Feed** (`/feed`)               | A bounded, verified snapshot of public Reddit conversations in curated faith, news, and civic communities. See the [Feed guide](/infolitico/the-feed).                       |
| **Archive** (`/archive`)             | Published editions listed by day. Each date links to that day's stories and story count.                                                                                     |

## Story pages

A published story lives at `/story/[slug]`. The page can include:

- the headline and summary;
- a curated verse when the story has one;
- the Infolitico Newsroom byline, publication time, estimated reading time, section, and linked person when one is present;
- an editorial image with its caption and available image attribution;
- the story body;
- the linked publisher source or source event;
- share controls, a trust and provenance card, and related stories.

A story keeps its source attribution visible when the page has a source
headline. If a correction or retraction notice applies, the story route shows
the editorial notice and links to the [corrections policy](/infolitico/policies#corrections-and-retractions).

## People hubs

The People index is available at `/people`. It lists public figures who have
published Infolitico stories. Each person has a hub at `/person/[slug]` with the
person's category, name, and latest related stories. The page is built around
that person's published Infolitico coverage rather than a separate biography.

## Move through an edition

1. Start at **Front Page** for the shared current edition.
2. Use a section desk when you want a particular subject area.
3. Open a story to read the source-grounded report and its reflection.
4. Follow the verse, person, source, or related-story links when you want more context.
5. Use **Archive** to return to an earlier publication day.
