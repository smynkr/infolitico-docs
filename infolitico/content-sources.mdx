The checked-in `RSS_FEEDS` configuration is a diet of direct publisher
RSS/Atom feeds. Since November 2026 no entry is routed through Google News:
former Google News site-search and topic entries now either use their
publisher's own feed or have been retired. It is a configuration snapshot,
not evidence that every feed is reachable from the ingestion host or appears
in the deployed edition — the watcher picks up a changed feed list only
after a redeploy.

## Configured source families

- **National and general news:** BBC, NPR, PBS NewsHour, CBS News, ABC News,
  USA Today, and Axios.
- **Business and technology:** Bloomberg, CNBC, The Wall Street Journal,
  TechCrunch, The Verge, WIRED, Ars Technica, Fortune, and Fast Company.
- **Culture:** NPR Books, NPR Music, NPR Culture, Variety, The Hollywood
  Reporter, and ArtNews.
- **Local, regional, and international:** The Texas Tribune, CalMatters,
  WBEZ, WBUR, WAMU, MinnPost, BBC World, Deutsche Welle, France 24, and
  Al Jazeera.
- **Faith, values, service, and public interest:** Christianity Today,
  Religion News Service, Christian Post, WORLD Magazine, Deseret News,
  Relevant, EWTN News, Christian Science Monitor, Smithsonian, Live Science,
  World Vision, Direct Relief, and Good News Network.

<Note>
  A November 2026 diet change
  ([PR #490](https://github.com/smynkr/infolitico/pull/490)) moved ten
  Google-News-routed sources to their publishers' own feeds under the same
  source keys and retired the rest: the AP and Reuters wire searches,
  Business Insider, The Information, the Miami Herald, The Seattle Times,
  Baptist Press, CBN News, and every Google News topic search. AP and
  Reuters cannot be fetched within policy — Reuters' robots.txt disallows
  generic crawlers and AP serves a bot challenge — and Google News summaries
  repeated only the headline, so such rows could never pass the admission
  bar below. The other retirements had no feed the watcher could use, or
  were a poor fit for the diet (paywalled or whole-site-only feeds). Retired
  keys may still appear inertly in a few configuration files; they produce
  no new source events.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Feed configuration is not live-health evidence. Merged
  [PR #426](https://github.com/smynkr/infolitico/pull/426) recorded that the
  ingestion host received a Cloudflare 403 from Smithsonian and had no
  `source_events` from it; ArtNews was verified as a Culture replacement from
  that host. The Tier-K Smithsonian entry remains in source configuration,
  so this page does not claim that it is currently supplying published
  stories.
</Warning>

## Admission into the pipeline

An event is admitted only when it carries real factual evidence:

- The source summary must be nonempty and more than the headline repeated.
- Feed chrome (such as "submitted by …", link lists, or comment counts) is
  never promoted into factual evidence.
- Rows without sufficient evidence are rejected before any model work, so
  junk rows never consume a generation slot.

## Discovery URLs and verified publisher URLs

The pipeline distinguishes between discovery URLs (where an event was found)
and verified publisher URLs (the canonical article location). Provenance is
carried through the pipeline so a published story's source is traceable.
Under the all-publisher diet, new events arrive with the publisher's own URL
from the start; the URL resolver still settles events that were discovered
earlier through Google News redirects.

## The reader feed is separate from ingestion

The public reader feed on the site is a publishing surface, not an ingestion
source. Ingestion and publishing are deliberately separate paths.
