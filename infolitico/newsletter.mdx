## Cadence and availability

Subscriber-facing confirmation copy describes a weekday email. The checked-in
systemd timer is configured for a daily 06:00 America/Los_Angeles run. A dated
operations record confirms one past run only, not the current schedule.

Thin mornings are skipped rather than padded with filler. This means an empty
or small slate is an intentional quality state, not a promise to send a
made-up edition.

## What goes in the digest

When digest delivery is active, its items are drawn from published Infolitico
stories:

- a concise selection of source-backed stories;
- the published Infolitico headline and a link to the Infolitico story;
- a link to the original source for each item; and
- the published facts and Scripture reused as-is rather than rewritten by an
  email model.

The sender skips a thin slate rather than padding it. Current production
behavior cannot be established from the source snapshot and dated run record.

## Subscribe and manage consent

1. Open the newsletter signup on
   [infolitico.com](https://infolitico.com) and enter your email address.
2. Select the explicit consent checkbox and submit the form.
3. Check your inbox and follow the confirmation link. The site does not send
   the morning email until you confirm.

Signup uses double opt-in. A submitted address is pending until the
confirmation link is used; the confirmation link expires after 24 hours.
Every message includes a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing suppresses
the address so it is not mailed again. Older signup records are not treated as
confirmed consent and must opt in again.

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://infolitico.com/privacy) for the consent
record, retention, aggregate engagement measurement, and delivery-processor
details.

## Browse the published archive

The public [Archive](https://infolitico.com/archive) lists every published
edition by day. Each date shows its story count and opens a day page containing
that day's published stories. Use the archive to revisit the source-backed
stories behind an edition even when you are not using email.
