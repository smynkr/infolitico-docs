Infolitico's editorial standards combine editorial commitments with automated
checks. Product code describes an automated source-event NEWS pipeline and a
separate owner-gated publisher for hand-authored Inspirational Thoughts. These
are distinct publishing paths; code and configuration do not establish which
path is currently active in production or whether a message was delivered.
Automated checks reduce risk for the patterns they recognize. They cannot prove
that every claim is true, catch every fabricated or inaccurate quotation, or
make publication infallible.

## Editorial commitments and controls

### 1. Source-based reporting

The automated NEWS pipeline begins with source events and applies evidence
checks before later generation steps. It carries source information for
provenance. This describes code behavior; it does not prove that a feed is
reachable, an event has been published, or every published claim is correct.

### 2. Scripture when selected

The NEWS pipeline selects verses from curated `bible_verses` data and carries
the stored reference and text as canonical values. Publication guards reject
detected changes to the selected verse and recognized unverified
quotation-marked Scripture or Bible citations outside the leading verse
block. These are bounded checks, not a guarantee that every reference,
quotation, or generated claim is error-free.

### 3. Source and commentary quotations

The pipeline supplies source and commentary quotations as input and checks the
quotes selected for an outline against those supplied records. Quotation
guards cover recognized forms; they are not a general fact-check of every
sentence and do not prove that no fabricated quotation can pass.

### 4. Protected-person checks

Product code includes checks against configured blocked persons, including a
pre-publish check in the owner-gated Inspirational Thought publisher. These
checks address the cases they recognize; they do not prove that every
protected-person case is detectable or that a publishing path is currently
active.

### 5. Content gates

The daily NEWS pipeline applies content scores, section requirements,
quotation checks, and a 250–550-word polished-body bound. The separate
owner-gated Thought publisher has its own validation, including a 700–900-word
polished-body bound. Rejection is expected when an implemented gate fails;
passing those checks is not independent verification of every fact.

## News first, faith second

The editorial intention is to lead with source-grounded reporting and add
spiritual reflection where there is a genuine faith connection. Automated
checks can reject candidates that fail their configured evidence or
faith-angle tests, but cannot guarantee that every article meets this standard
perfectly.

## Editorial voice and length targets

- **Warm and conversational** — a thoughtful friend sharing a morning
  devotional, not a pastor at a pulpit.
- **Values, not politics** — family, personal responsibility, stewardship,
  gratitude, character, community, and faith in God's plan; no party
  endorsements or left/right framing.
- **Earnest, constructive, and dignifying** — treat people with care; avoid
  mockery or humiliation.
- **Short and accessible** — writing guidance targets 300–500 words for a
  daily reflection. This is an editorial target, not the pipeline's exact
  acceptance range or a publication guarantee.

<Warning>
  Automated checks are safeguards, not proof of infallibility. If you spot a
  factual or Scripture error, see the [Corrections](https://infolitico.com/retractions)
  page for how to report it.
</Warning>
